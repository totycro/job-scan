Example Semiconductor Vietnam is looking forward to working with a technical talent in the field of Hardware Validation for Automotive Ethernet Solutions.

Job Description
In your new role you will:
  • Analyze technical standard speciﬁcation for critical implementation detail
  • Develop test plan and perform conformance testing on bench to ensure device is compliant to technical standard speciﬁcation
  • Debug and assist in resolving bench identiﬁed issue
  • Support on the design and implementation of test platform required to perform conformance testing
  • Develop software tool to utilize the test platform as well as post-process conformance test result


Your Profile
You are best equipped for this task if you have:
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering or related ﬁelds and 3-5 years of related professional experience
  • Master’s degree and/or PhD in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering or related ﬁelds with 2-3 years of experience
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English
  • Experience in silicon bench testing and debug
  • Proﬁciency in C/C++ with experience on test equipment automation
  • Good technical reading skill for understanding technical speciﬁcation and manual is needed.
  • Familiarity with lab equipment such as oscilloscope, FPGA usage is desired.
  • Familiarity with Ethernet transceivers and associated standards IEEE 802.3 is desired.
