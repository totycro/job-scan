# Project instructions

Read and follow the [project constitution](.specify/memory/constitution.md) before
planning or making changes. It is the source of truth for project-wide policies.

Do not copy internet content into tracked repository files, including webpage captures,
job descriptions, or identifying site/company details in fixtures, filenames, code, or docs.
Keep raw captures and real evaluation URLs in ignored local storage such as `.local/`.
Use authored synthetic examples or anonymized, rephrased examples in the repository;
taking inspiration from an online example and rewriting it is fine. Existing anonymized
evaluation fixtures may remain. Links to technical documentation are fine.
